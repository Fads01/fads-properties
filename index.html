Our story

We started FADS Properties because good people kept getting a bad deal

Too many buyers, renters and landlords in Nigeria have a story about a listing that didn't exist, a title that wasn't clean, or an agent who disappeared after collecting a fee. We started FADS Properties to be the exception — a real estate company that treats verification and follow-through as the whole job, not an afterthought.

Today we work with individuals, families, investors, landlords and businesses across Lagos, Abuja and Port Harcourt, on everything from a first rental flat to multi-unit investment portfolios.

Our mission

Make property decisions safe, clear and fair

We exist to remove the uncertainty from buying, renting, selling and managing property in Nigeria — for everyone from a first-time renter to an experienced investor.

FAQ

Common questions

Do you charge buyers or renters to view listings?+

No. Browsing and enquiring about listings on our site is free. Standard agency fees apply only once a transaction proceeds, and we disclose them upfront.

How do you verify a property before listing it?+

An agent physically inspects the property and we confirm the relevant ownership or allocation documents before it's published on our site.

Which cities do you currently cover?+

We're active in Lagos, Abuja and Port Harcourt, with select opportunities in other growing markets on request.

Can you manage a property I already own?+

Yes. Our property management service covers tenant screening, rent collection, maintenance and reporting for existing landlords.

Do you work with businesses and organisations, not just individuals?+

Yes — we handle commercial leases, corporate housing and portfolio management for companies and organisations as well as individuals.
